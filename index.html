Ground & visual provenance

This is a newly authored fantasy layout, not an exact Inverness reconstruction. The retained government ground has been adapted into the undistorted illustration frame. Source terrain, earlier course and previous builds are preserved.

The photographic-looking overview uses the approved generated landscape. Close short grass combines that macro appearance with procedural native-scale turf detail. This is not a newly photographed high-resolution green; the full landscape retains its original macro-detail limit.

Visible playing surfaces are authored in the same coordinates as targeting, terrain inspection and physics. Obscured creek banks and tree crowns remain approximate. Branch/leaf collision physics and empirical flight/turf calibration remain future work.
